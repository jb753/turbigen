turbigen is a general turbomachinery design system developed by Dr. James Brind of the Whittle Laboratory, University of Cambridge.

This documentation contains usage instructions, descriptions of the theory involved, and listings of configuration options.

Publications using turbigen:

  • Brind, J., “Data-driven radial compressor design space mapping”. J. Turbomach. [Brind, 2024].

  • Torres-Gomez, A., Brind, J., and Pullan, G. “Cryogenic Radial Turbine Design for High-Efficiency Hydrogen Liquefaction Plants”. ASME Turbo Expo 2025 [A. Torres-Gomez and Pullan, 2025].
